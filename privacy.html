MacroLoom Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026 · Applies to the MacroLoom app for Android (com.macroloom.app) and iOS (com.macroloom.app), version 1.0.0 and later.

MacroLoom is a macro and weight tracking app. You log what you eat, set daily macro targets and record your weight. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, which service providers are involved, how long data is kept and how you can delete it. It is written to match what the app actually does; if we change what the app does, we update this page and its date.

1. Who is responsible

MacroLoom is developed and published by Matias Myllymäki, a private individual resident in Finland, who is the data controller for the processing described here. MacroLoom is not operated by a company.

Privacy contact: macroloom.app@gmail.com

This mailbox is the contact channel for every request under this policy. We do not publish a postal address or telephone number; where a competent authority or the law requires further contact details, we provide them on request.

2. Summary

  • You need an account to use MacroLoom. You sign in with Google or Apple (these are the only sign-in methods the app offers); the app never sees or stores a password.
  • Everything you track (foods, meals, macro targets, weight) is stored in a local database on your device and synced to your account on our server so that it survives reinstalling and can move to a new phone.
  • Paid access is handled by Google Play or the App Store, with RevenueCat checking your purchase. We never receive card or bank details.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no selling or sharing of data for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time, or by e-mail. See Delete Your Account.
  • MacroLoom is for adults only (18+).

4. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a local SQLite database on your device with your food library, log, periods, weight history, settings, reminder and theme preferences, a queue of changes waiting to sync, the sync status described above and, during an account deletion, a progress marker. This database belongs to the app's private storage; other apps cannot read it.

Your sign-in session is stored encrypted: the session itself is encrypted with a key that is kept in the device's secure key store (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). A small "offline access" record that lets the app keep working for a while without a network connection is kept in the same secure store and is tied to this device only.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this from the device. It does not delete your account (see Delete Your Account).

5. Data stored in your account (cloud sync)

When you are signed in and online, the app uploads your food library, log, periods, weight history and macro/reset settings to your account on our server, and restores them when you install MacroLoom on a new device. Sync is one device at a time: the device you most recently chose to "use" is the active one, and other devices become read-only until you switch.

Each record on the server is tagged with your account UUID. Access rules on the database (row-level security) only ever let a signed-in user read their own rows, and all changes go through server functions that check the caller's identity before writing. The app contains no administrative credentials; only a public project address and publishable key, which on their own grant no access to anyone's data.

Reminders (weigh-in notifications) are scheduled locally on your device. MacroLoom does not use push notifications and never registers a push token.

8. What MacroLoom does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. (Google Play and Apple may still show us anonymous crash and install statistics from their own platforms.)
  • No location data, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No push notifications and no push tokens; reminders are scheduled on your device only.
  • No passwords, and no e-mail or magic-link sign-in: the app offers Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple only.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing, and no marketing e-mails.

9. App permissions

On Android the app requests or declares the following permissions. Some are declared automatically by libraries the app is built with and are not used by MacroLoom itself.

  • Internet and network state — signing in, syncing your account and checking purchases.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — asked only when you turn the weigh-in reminder on. Boot completed, vibrate, wake lock — so a scheduled reminder survives a restart and can alert you.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.
  • Biometric / fingerprint — declared by the secure-storage library; MacroLoom does not ask for a fingerprint or face unlock.
  • Read / write external storage (Android 12 and earlier only) — declared by a library; MacroLoom does not read or write your files.
  • Launcher badge permissions for various phone brands — declared by the notification library so an app badge count can be shown.

10. Retention and deletion

  • While you have an account: your tracking data and settings are kept so that the app can show and sync them. Finished tracking periods remain in your History until you delete your account.
  • Account deletion in the app: deleting your account from Settings removes your authentication record and, in the same transaction, every row that belongs to your account on our server: foods, periods, entries, weight entries, settings, device-sync status, trial/access status and the import ledger. Your RevenueCat customer record is then deleted through RevenueCat's API; this is retried until RevenueCat confirms it.
  • Account deletion by e-mail: we carry out the same deletion manually from our server administration console, which removes your authentication record and every row that belongs to your account, and we delete your RevenueCat customer record manually. We complete e-mail requests within 30 days of receiving them, which is within the one-month period the GDPR allows (Art. 12(3)).
  • What remains after an in-app deletion: a deletion record holding only the account's UUID, the deletion time and the cleanup status. It contains no e-mail address, name, tracking data or purchase data and exists so that the RevenueCat customer record is verifiably removed and never recreated by an old device. It is kept indefinitely, on the basis of our legitimate interest in completing and proving the deletion (Art. 6(1)(f)); without the deleted account data it cannot be linked back to you. Deletions carried out manually after an e-mail request do not create this record.
  • Store subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your account; cancel them in Google Play or your Apple ID settings.
  • Backups and logs: on our current hosting plan (Supabase Free) there are no automated database backups of our project, so deleted data does not live on in a backup that we hold or could restore. Platform logs (API, database and authentication logs) are retained for one day and then expire. If we move to a plan that includes backups, we will state the backup retention period here.
  • E-mails you send us (including deletion requests) are kept as long as needed to handle your request and to show that we handled it.

11. Security

Measures actually in place in the app and on the server:

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS. The app instructs the device never to cache responses from our server.
  • Database row-level security limits every read to the signed-in user's own rows; all writes go through server functions that verify the caller's identity on the server before touching any data, and that refuse writes from a device that is not the account's active device.
  • No administrative or secret keys are shipped in the app. Server-side secrets (for the database, RevenueCat and Apple) exist only in server configuration.
  • Account deletion can only be requested for the account that owns the current session, and only after that session has been verified with the authentication server.
  • On your device, the sign-in session is encrypted with a key held in the system secure store, and the offline-access record is bound to that device.
  • Diagnostic logging in the app is limited to fixed status messages; it never writes tokens, e-mail addresses, identifiers or tracking data.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been accessed without authorisation, contact us at the address below.

  • Supabase states that its platform logs are stored in EU data centres, but its account, billing and support systems and some of its sub-processors operate in the United States and Singapore, and its contracting entity is Supabase Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). Transfers out of the EU/EEA are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914) incorporated in the Supabase Data Processing Addendum, supported by Supabase's transfer impact assessment. See also Supabase's data residency and transfers FAQ.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. operates in the United States on Amazon Web Services, so your account UUID and purchase status are transferred to the US. The RevenueCat Data Processing Addendum incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses (module two, controller to processor) and the UK addendum.
  • Google LLC (Sign in with Google, Google Play Billing) is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and also relies on Standard Contractual Clauses, as described on Google's data-transfer frameworks page.
  • Apple: for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Apple Distribution International Limited (Ireland) is the controller for Sign in with Apple and the App Store, and Apple's privacy policy states that its international transfers are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses.

The links above lead to the providers' own documents, which may change; we check them when we update this policy.

13. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or another jurisdiction with similar rights, you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we process your data and give you a copy of it (access and portability);
  • correct inaccurate data (most of your data you can edit directly in the app);
  • delete your data (see Delete Your Account);
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning the reminder off or deleting entries; this does not affect processing that already happened.

To exercise a right, e-mail macroloom.app@gmail.com from the address you use to sign in. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account before acting. We answer within one month, as the GDPR requires (Art. 12(3)); for unusually complex or numerous requests this can be extended by up to two further months, and we tell you within the first month if that is the case. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Finnish Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto). If you live or work in another EU/EEA country, you may also complain to the authority there.

Because we hold no card details and run no marketing, there is no automated decision-making or profiling to object to.

14. Age requirement

MacroLoom is a diet and weight tracking tool intended only for adults aged 18 and older, and it is listed on Google Play for an audience of 18+. It is not directed to children or teenagers. The app does not technically verify your age; by creating an account you confirm that you are at least 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it. If you believe this has happened, contact us at the address below.
